## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a quiet, high-end editorial presence for a contemporary ceramics atelier. The digital experience translates the tactile, deliberate slowness of hand-thrown pottery into screen space.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Intentional & Serene:** Prioritizes unhurried whitespace, generous line spacing, and balanced compositions that invite focused appreciation rather than rapid scanning.
- **Rooted in Provenance:** Blends traditional European craftsmanship nuances with modern architectural clarity, signaling heirloom-level quality, material honesty, and cultural depth.
- **Sensory & Earthy:** Grounded by mineral pigments, porous textures, and subtle structural frames reminiscent of wood drying racks, stone plinths, and raw terra-cotta slip.

### Design Aesthetic: Tactile Editorial Craftsmanship
The design marries an editorial layout rhythm with organic ceramic subtleties. Surfaces are not flat digital white; they carry the warmth of unbleached linen and bisqueware. Structural boundaries utilize ultra-thin, low-contrast hairline dividers that echo precision joinery and wire clay cutters.

## Colors

The palette directly references the studio's physical materials: raw iron slips, unglazed stoneware, calcined clay, and weathered glazes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#183B46` — Deep Heritage Blue):** Used for focal headlines, primary action surfaces, strong anchor elements, and primary navigation states. Imparts architectural weight and historical authority.
- **Secondary (`#B86F52` — Earthy Clay Terracotta):** Reserved for deliberate points of intention: badges, interactive hover states, editorial accents, and select transactional highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#A4AA91` — Muted Sage):** Represents dry ash glaze; applied to soft status badges, secondary interactive selections, and quiet metadata highlights.
- **Neutral (`#D8C7AC` — Sand Beige):** The structural mid-tone. Powers secondary surfaces, card backgrounds, elevated containers, and structural dividers.
- **Canvas (`#F4EFE5` — Warm Ivory):** The default application surface. Emulates raw porcelain and natural paper, softening visual strain and establishing ambient warmth.
- **Ink (`#292B28` — Studio Charcoal):** Deep mineral black with an olive undertone. Used for long-form reading, fine captions, and form inputs to avoid the harshness of pure black.

### Application Rules
- Maintain high contrast ratios by pairing Ink (`#292B28`) or Primary (`#183B46`) directly over Warm Ivory (`#F4EFE5`) and Sand Beige (`#D8C7AC`).
- Use Earthy Clay Terracotta sparingly; treat it as an authentic studio seal or potter's mark rather than an omnipresent fill color.

## Typography

The typographic pairing contrasts high-elegance editorial serif headlines with a disciplined, humanistic sans-serif for interface precision.

### Hierarchy & Composition
- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the permanence and grace of artisanal craft. Generous line heights allow multi-line editorial titles to breathe. Italic styles can be introduced selectively within editorial hero titles to emphasize craft terms, collection names, or dimensions.
- **Interface & Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, neutral, and structurally open. It balances the dramatic serif with legible, contemporary clarity in product specifications, operational dashboards, and purchasing flows.
- **Labeling Style:** `label-lg` and `label-sm` use tracked, capitalized configurations for collection tags, vessel capacities, glaze types, and table headers to evoke exhibition catalog labeling.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric editorial grid system engineered around negative space, treating the screen like an open exhibition floor.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of `1440px`. Column gutters set to `gutter-lg` (`2.5rem`), outer boundaries to `margin-lg` (`4rem`). Asymmetric splits (e.g., 5-column description paired with 7-column imagery) are encouraged to evoke gallery display stands.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin adjusts to `margin-md` (`2.5rem`), and gutters scale to `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin` (`1.25rem`) and `gutter` (`1.5rem`). Vertical stacking respects generous section padding (`space-2xl`) to avoid a cluttered commercial appearance.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards and dialogs utilize `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on desktop to maintain perimeter airiness, shrinking to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on mobile.
- **Section Pacing:** Group related data tightly with `space-xs` and `space-sm`, but isolate distinct functional clusters with `space-xl` and `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects harsh artificial drop shadows in favor of tonal layering and soft, low-contrast hairline boundaries.

### Depth Strategy
- **Base Level (Canvas):** `#F4EFE5` (Warm Ivory) establishes the broad, grounding backdrop.
- **Elevated Level 1 (Card & Section Insets):** `#D8C7AC` (Sand Beige) applied at 35% to 60% opacity over the base canvas, or solid `#EFE8DA` for structured plinth panels.
- **Elevated Level 2 (Floating Sheets, Drawers, Modals):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered with a warm, ambient earthen halo: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(24, 59, 70, 0.08)`. The tinted shadow avoids cool gray tones and subtly introduces the deep heritage blue tone.
- **Boundary Precision:** Structural frames utilize 1px solid hairline dividers colored with `#292B28` at 12% opacity (`rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.12)`). This provides structural definition without visual heaviness.

## Shapes

The geometric framework mirrors hand-shaped stoneware: soft, organic curves that feel turned on a potter’s wheel rather than mechanically stamped.

### Curvature Mapping
- Standard controls (buttons, inputs, chips) employ a soft curve (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`).
- Object containers, showcase product cards, and feature frames transition to broader radii (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / `rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), echoing smooth amphora and bowl profiles.
- Imagery can feature bespoke asymmetric arch masks (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 0.5rem 0.5rem`) on key editorial heroes to emulate classical kiln entryways and studio niches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#183B46` fill, `#F4EFE5` label text (`label-lg`), `0.5rem` border-radius. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to `#B86F52` (Clay Terracotta) with an ease-out timing curve (200ms).
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1px border styled with `rgba(24, 59, 70, 0.3)`, transparent background, `#183B46` text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(216, 199, 172, 0.3)`.
- **Text Action:** Clean, tracked uppercase label with an active underline pinned 4px below the text, rendered in `#B86F52`.

### Cards & Plinths
- **Product & Story Cards:** Base fill using semi-translucent Sand Beige (`rgba(216, 199, 172, 0.4)`) with a 1px border in `rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.08)`. Radius set to `1rem`. Internal padding of `1.5rem`.
- **Image Treatment within Cards:** Media containers adopt a slightly warmer background tint (`#EBE4D5`) to keep unglazed pottery photography cohesive against the ivory canvas.

### Chips & Tags
- **Attribute Badges (Clay, Glaze, Technique):** Compact heights (28px–32px), `0.5rem` radius. Tinted with Tertiary Sage (`rgba(164, 170, 145, 0.25)`) with `#292B28` text, or solid `#D8C7AC` at 50% opacity.
- **Selection Chips:** Unselected states feature a subtle 1px border. Selected states fill with `#183B46` and white text.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Canvas matches `#F4EFE5` or pure `#FFFFFF` when nested. 1px hairline border in `rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.2)`. Active focus state transitions the border to `#183B46` with an accompanying subtle outline ring in `rgba(24, 59, 70, 0.15)`. No loud system colors.
- **Placeholder Text:** Rendered in `rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.45)` with regular weights.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-sm`, uppercase, with letter-spacing `0.08em`, positioned strictly above the input.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circle controls. Inactive border: `1.5px solid rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.35)`.
- Active state fills with `#183B46` with a crisp `#F4EFE5` checkmark or center pip.

### Lists & Specifications
- Studio spec sheets (height, diameter, firing temp) use horizontal split rows separated by low-contrast hairline borders (`rgba(41, 43, 40, 0.1)`). Spec keys are set in `body-sm` Charcoal, values in `title-md` Deep Heritage Blue.
